# Term gate: is `pairing-conditioned` occupied

Run: 2026-08-11, before the root commit and before any quantity of the family
was computed. Recorded here in the third commit because the root commit holds
the preregistration and nothing else.

This is a term gate, not a literature review. It asks one narrow question:
is the name already carrying a meaning that would collide with the one this
lane intends. It does not survey the field, it does not establish priority,
and it does not begin the background review, which remains unstarted. See
`BACKGROUND-REVIEW.md`.

## Method and its limits, stated before the results

- One engine, one pass, English only, executed 2026-08-11.
- Queries are declared verbatim below and were fixed before their results
  were read. No query was added after seeing a result in order to improve the
  verdict.
- Not run, and therefore not claimed: a full text search of arXiv, a Google
  Scholar pass, a MathSciNet or zbMATH lookup, a search in any language other
  than English, a check of trademark or package name registries.
- Because of the above, a negative finding here means "not found by these
  queries", and never "does not exist".

## Declared queries

- Q1: `"pairing-conditioned" statistics term`
- Q2: `"pairing conditioned" null model permutation test`
- Q3: `"pairing-preserving" OR "pairing-constrained" permutation null model combinatorics`
- Q4: `"pairing-conditioned" arxiv`
- Q5: `"conditioned on the pairing" permutation null hypothesis sequence order statistic`

## What came back

### The exact compound

No result under Q1, Q2 or Q4 used `pairing-conditioned` as a hyphenated
technical compound, in statistics or anywhere else. Q4, restricted to arXiv,
returned no paper containing the compound; its top hits matched the two words
separately in unrelated senses. On these queries the compound is unoccupied.

### The collision that does exist

The two words together are heavily occupied in the psychology of learning.
Q1 and Q2, run in a statistical framing, returned as their dominant hits
material on classical and evaluative conditioning: conditioned stimulus and
unconditioned stimulus, pairing procedures, temporal overlap in evaluative
conditioning, conditioned reinforcers. The engine treated `pairing` plus
`conditioned` as that vocabulary and had to be pushed off it.

This is the finding of the gate. The compound is free, but the phrase sits in
the retrieval basin of a large and unrelated literature. A reader meeting the
title cold, and more to the point a search engine indexing it, will be pulled
toward stimulus conditioning.

### Occupied neighbours in the intended sense

These terms are taken, and mean specific things that are not what this lane
means. They are recorded so that the lane does not drift into them:

- `paired-permutation test`: standard, and it means permuting labels within
  pairs of observations. Not this. This lane permutes blocks and orientations
  of one object.
- `constrained permutation` and `null-model constraints`: occupied in
  network and community ecology null models.
- `non-exchangeable null models`: occupied, and technically adjacent, since
  the family of this lane is exactly a non-exchangeable null.
- `pairing-preserving`: used descriptively in expositions of paired
  permutation tests. Not coined as a term of art, but not free of association
  either.

### The vocabulary of the source this lane depends on

Radisic (2026), arXiv:2601.07175, titles the object a matching, not a
pairing: "Optimal Equivariant Matchings on the 6-Cube". The result this lane
conditions on is stated there in the language of matchings.

## Verdict

`pairing-conditioned` is available as a coined term. It is not occupied.

It is nonetheless the weaker of the two candidates, for two reasons that are
independent of each other: it collides in retrieval with the conditioning
literature, and it does not use the noun that the source result uses.

`matching-conditioned` avoids both. It inherits the vocabulary of the cited
theorem and it leaves the psychology basin. Its cost is that `pairing` is the
word an I Ching reader would recognize for the thirty-two pairs.

## Status of the decision

DECIDED. `matching-conditioned` is adopted.

- Date: 2026-08-11, in session two.
- Decider: Alexis Garcia Hurtado.
- Grounds: both of the two the gate reported, and they are independent of
  each other. The compound sits in the retrieval basin of the psychology of
  conditioning, and the source theorem calls the object a matching rather
  than a pairing.
- Recorded in `DECISIONS.md`, decision one.

Everything above this section is the measurement that produced the
recommendation, and it stays as it was written before the decision was taken.
It is not rewritten to agree with the outcome, and in particular the verdict
above still says what it said: that `pairing-conditioned` was available and
unoccupied. It was. It was adopted against, on grounds of collision and of
vocabulary, not on grounds of occupancy.

The rename cost nothing in the design. The preregistration defines the family
extensionally and uses no name as a load bearing term, so no signed text
needed amending, which is why the gate was run before anything was fixed.
