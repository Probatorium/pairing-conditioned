# Third party manifest

Every file this lane did not produce and that has entered the working copy is
listed here, under rule five of `CONTACT-RULES.md`. Entries are written at
the moment of entry. A file present under `vendor/` and absent from this
table is a rule violation, not an oversight.

Required for each entry: file name, origin, exact retrieval address,
retrieval date, SHA-256 digest, licence or rights status as stated by the
source, and the use this lane makes of it.

## Entries

None. No third party file has entered this repository.

## Known future entry

The analysis needs a table of the sixty-four hexagrams giving, for each one,
its King Wen position and its six line values. This lane does not own such a
table and will not invent one. When it enters it comes in under this
manifest, with its digest recorded and checked on every run, and with its
line convention stated explicitly rather than inferred.

Two properties of that table will be checked against the primary source
before any use, and the check is named here in advance so that it cannot be
skipped quietly: that the table has sixty-four distinct hexagrams, and that
its adjacency pairing is the matching M of the preregistration. The second of
these is the precondition gate P1 of preregistration section (d), and it can
stop the lane.
