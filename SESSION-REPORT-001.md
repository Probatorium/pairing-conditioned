## What the preregistration commits this lane to, in brief

The family is the set of sequences realising the King Wen pairing, generated
by a permutation of the thirty-two blocks and an orientation for each. The
statistic is the discordance count against the binary order, in four bit
conventions, decomposed into an orientation component with an exact law and a
block order component. Four tests per distinct convention, Holm corrected,
tails fixed in advance. Sample size and seed fixed. A precondition gate that
can stop the lane before any test runs.

The refutation criterion was checked for failability before signing, in both
directions, and the check is written into the document. Refutation is
reachable and is the expected outcome. Survival is reachable, and the
concrete conditions under which it would occur are stated in advance so that
they can be held against the result.
